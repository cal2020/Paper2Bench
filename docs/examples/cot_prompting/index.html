   - **Procedure**: Use the GSM8K dataset with 1,319 examples. Implement chain-of-thought prompting with 8 exemplars formatted as ⟨input, chain of thought, output⟩. Each exemplar should follow the structure shown in the paper, where the model is prompted to reason through the problem step-by-step.
   - **What to Record**: Record the solve rate (percentage of correct answers) for each model. Capture the raw outputs and the generated chains of thought for analysis.
   - **Correspondence**: Figure 2, Table 1, Section 3.2.

## Evaluation Details

- **Metrics**: 
  - Solve rate (%) = (Number of correct answers / Total number of questions) * 100.
  - Use the `accuracy_score` function from the `sklearn.metrics` library to compute the solve rate.

- **Normalization/Pre-processing**: 
  - Ensure that all model outputs are stripped of leading/trailing whitespace and converted to a consistent case (e.g., lowercase) before scoring.

- **Aggregation Strategy**: 
  - Calculate the solve rate per sample, then aggregate results per condition (dataset/model) to compute overall performance metrics.

## Implementation Notes

- **Key Pitfalls**: 
  - Ensure that the chain-of-thought exemplars are correctly formatted and relevant to the specific dataset to avoid misleading the model.
  - Be aware of the potential for models to generate incorrect reasoning paths, which may lead to correct answers by chance.

- **Prompt Templates**: 
  - Use the specific prompt templates provided in the paper for each task, ensuring that the structure ⟨input, chain of thought, output⟩ is maintained.

- **Order of Operations**: 
  - Follow the sequence of tasks as outlined in the experimental design, ensuring that each model is evaluated under the same conditions for consistency.
