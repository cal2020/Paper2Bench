2. **Task 2: Learning Curve Analysis on rMD17 Dataset**
   - **Procedure**: Train MACE models with varying correlation orders (ν = 1, 2, 3) on the rMD17 dataset. Use 1000 configurations (950 for training and 50 for validation). The models should be trained with a radial cutoff of 5 Å.

   - **Procedure**: Evaluate MACE models trained on the 3BPA dataset at different temperatures (300 K, 600 K, 1200 K) and on dihedral slices. Use the same training configurations as in Task 1.
   - **Calculation Type**: Testing of ML interatomic potentials.

   - **Correspondence**: Table 2, Section 5.3.

4. **Task 4: Benchmarking MACE on AcAc Dataset**
   - **Procedure**: Train MACE models on the AcAc dataset with 500 configurations (450 for training and 50 for validation). Use a radial cutoff of 5 Å.
   - **Calculation Type**: Training and validation of ML interatomic potentials.
   - **What to Record**: RMSE on energies and forces at 300 K and 600 K.
   - **Correspondence**: Table 3, Section 5.3.

## Evaluation Details

- **Metrics Definitions**:
  - **MAE**: Mean Absolute Error calculated as \( \text{MAE} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} |E_i - \hat{E}_i| \) for energies and \( \text{MAE} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} |F_i - \hat{F}_i| \) for forces, where \( E_i \) and \( F_i \) are the true values, and \( \hat{E}_i \) and \( \hat{F}_i \) are the predicted values.
  - **Learning Curve Steepness**: Analyzed by fitting a power law to the log-log plot of predictive error as a function of dataset size.

- **Post-Processing**:
  - For learning curves, average the RMSE over multiple runs to obtain stable estimates.
  - Ensure k-point convergence for DFT calculations if applicable, using a grid of [uncertain] points.
  - Apply a smearing technique with a width of [uncertain] eV for electronic structure calculations.

- **Aggregation Strategy**:
  - Compute metrics per structure, then average over all structures to obtain per-composition metrics, and finally aggregate to overall metrics.

## Implementation Notes

- **Key Pitfalls**:
  - Ensure that the radial cutoff is consistently set to 5 Å across all models to avoid discrepancies in performance.
  - Monitor convergence thresholds for training, particularly for the learning rate and batch size, to prevent overfitting.
